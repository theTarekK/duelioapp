How to play iMessage games

With Duelio on iPhone and iPad

Duelio is a collection of 30 iMessage games you can launch from a conversation in Apple's Messages app. Challenge a friend to pool or chess, trade Word Hunt scores, or invite your group chat to a multiplayer game. No ads or sign-up required.

Open the Duelio iMessage extension

  1. Download Duelio Games from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Open Messages and choose an iMessage conversation with a friend or group.
  3. Tap the + button beside the message field. Swipe up to find more apps, then choose Duelio. On older versions, look in the app drawer above the keyboard.
  4. Choose a game, configure the available options, and send the game invitation into your chat.
  5. Your friends open the invitation to play. Each player needs Duelio installed on a supported iPhone or iPad.

The extension is the part of Duelio that opens inside Messages. The same download also includes a standalone app for online rooms, friends, tournaments and more.

Choose a game to play with friends

  • Sports: challenge friends to Pool, Darts, Bowling and more.
  • Strategy: take your time with Chess, Checkers or Four in a Row.
  • Word games: try Word Hunt, Anagrams and Word Shift.
  • Multiplayer: bring friends together for Word Bomb, Trivia Rush, Poker and other live games.
  • Group chat games: Insider and 2 Truths & 1 Lie are iMessage-only games for at least three participants.

Browse the complete Duelio game collection or watch the gameplay previews.

Take turns or play live

Turn-based iMessage games let you take a turn and send it back through the conversation, so you and your friend can play at different times. Live multiplayer games use a ready room and run in real time, so gather the players before starting. Available player counts and options depend on the game.

iMessage games: common questions

Why can't I find Duelio in Messages?

Check that Duelio is installed on your device, then open an iMessage conversation and tap +. Swipe up through the app list to look for Duelio. For help finding and rearranging apps, see Apple's iMessage apps guide. If it still does not appear, contact Duelio support.

Can I play Duelio's iMessage games on Android?

Duelio is currently available on iPhone and iPad. Its Messages extension uses Apple's iMessage. Android and browser versions are in progress.

Can I play without sending an iMessage?

Yes. Open the standalone Duelio app from your Home Screen and use Host Online, Find a Game or Join with Code. Insider and 2 Truths & 1 Lie are available only in the iMessage extension.

Does Duelio have ads or require an account?

No ads or sign-up are required to play. Duelio also offers optional Pro, cosmetics and other in-app purchases. See the Duelio Shop for the available extras.
