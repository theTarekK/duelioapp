Good to know

Website convenience. Apple checkout.

Can the website see what I own?

No. Your Apple purchase history is private. When Duelio opens, StoreKit checks the App Store account signed in on that device and updates the item there.

Will I be charged twice?

Duelio blocks repurchasing non-consumable items it already recognizes. Apple’s purchase sheet always shows what will happen before you confirm.

Why does checkout open the game?

That keeps purchases connected to your Apple account and uses the same trusted StoreKit checkout as the in-game shop.
