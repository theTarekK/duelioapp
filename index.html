Why it hits different

Built inside the conversation

💬

Plays where you talk

Duelio lives in the iMessage app drawer. An invite is just a message — tap the bubble and you’re in the game. No links, no lobbies to hunt for.

♟️

Turn-based duels

Your moves travel inside the conversation itself. Take your turn on your own time — chess, pool, darts, bowling, word games and more.

⚡

Live multiplayer

Ready rooms, real-time play and in-game chat across 11 games — drawing, trivia, cards, Word Bomb, Landmark and more. Everyone in the chat can join.

🎨

Make it yours

Design custom pool tables and boards, draw your own Tic-Tac-Toe symbols, and unlock characters, badges and cues as you play.

🌍

Four languages

The word games ship with full English, Spanish, French and Italian dictionaries — pick per game, right from the invite.

🔒

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no permissions — and turn-based games never touch a server at all. Read the policy →

The fine print, unfined

We built Duelio to need as little of your data as possible.

  • No third-party software — zero ads, analytics or trackers
  • No accounts, no passwords, no permissions requested
  • Your stats and unlocks never leave your device
  • Turn-based games ride iMessage — we never see them

One honest caveat: live matches connect to a Duelio server and are not end-to-end encrypted. We say so plainly, in plain English.
