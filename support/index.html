Support

Quick answers first — and a human at the bottom.

Where is Duelio? I installed it but don't see an app icon.

Duelio lives inside Messages. Open a conversation, tap the + (or the app drawer strip above the keyboard), and pick Duelio. The full game catalog opens right there — choose a game and send the invite into the conversation.

What's the difference between turn-based and live games?

Turn-based games (chess, pool, darts, word games and more) travel inside the conversation itself — take your turn, send it, and the bubble updates for everyone. Play whenever you like.

Live games (Draw, Trivia Rush, Word Bomb, the card games and more) open a real-time match with a ready room and in-game chat. Everyone in the conversation can hop in while the match is running.

How do I report a player or something someone sent or drew?

Email contact@metkapps.com with what you saw and when. We aim to review reports of objectionable content and abusive behaviour, and act on them, within 24 hours.

For turn-based games the message travels through iMessage itself, so Apple's own tools are the effective ones there: block the sender in Messages and report the message to Apple.

Purchases, restores and refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment details. To request a refund, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com.

Duelio Pro and hints are tied to your device and shared between the app and its iMessage extension automatically.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Your stats, unlocks, settings and name are stored on your device only, and deleting Duelio deletes all of it. Live-match data on our server expires on its own — usually within about four hours of a match being abandoned. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
